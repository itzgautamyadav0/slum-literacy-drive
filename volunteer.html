Volunteer Teacher Shift Registration

Join our grassroots cohort of evening volunteer teachers. Give just 1 hour an evening to educate and mentor underprivileged children in urban slums.

Teacher Volunteer Perks

You don't need to be a certified educator. If you can patiently teach basic reading, counting, or sciences, our slum camps need your guidance.

Social Internship Certificate

Get a verified certificate of completion (25+ teaching hours) recognized for university credits and resumes.

100% Flexible Evening Shifts

Choose 1-hour slots (4-5 PM, 5-6 PM, 6-7 PM) and specific days that easily match your college timetable.

Free Teaching Materials Provided

We provide curated flashcards, workbooks, abacus counters, and DIY science kits at each slum center.

Direct Admin Submission

Your application is submitted directly to the center coordinator's administrative panel for verification.
