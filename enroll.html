Child Admission & Evening Class Enrollment

Enroll underprivileged children living in urban slum clusters for completely free evening tutoring classes. Textbooks, stationery, and learning kits are provided at zero cost.

No fees of any kind. Every child receives free notebooks, pencil boxes, storybooks, and individualized volunteer teaching.

Convenient Evening Timings

Classes run Monday to Saturday from 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM so children who assist parents during the day can learn every evening.
